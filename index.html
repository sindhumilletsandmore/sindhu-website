Our Story

Bringing millets back to the family table

Sindhu Millets & More began with a simple belief — the traditional grains our grandparents thrived on deserve a place in every modern home. We sprout and roast each grain the slow, natural way to unlock better taste and easy nutrition.

No preservatives, no added sugar — just honest, wholesome food. From everyday java powders to instant idli & dosa mixes, every product is made for the whole family.
